# PicknPlay Game Rules

## 1. Game overview

PicknPlay is a shedding card game. Players take turns matching the suit or
rank of the top card on the discard pile, while using special cards to
change the flow of play or make opponents draw cards.

There are two variants:

-   **PicknPlay Classic:** played with a standard 52-card deck, without
    Jokers.
-   **PicknPlay Super:** played with a 54-card deck, including two Jokers.

In either variant, a player can win by being the first to play all the
cards in their hand. A player may also end the game by declaring
**"cut"** with the designated 7. When a cut is declared, the player with
the lowest hand-point total wins.

## 2. PicknPlay Classic

### 2.1 Equipment and setup

1.  Use a standard 52-card deck. Remove both Jokers.
2.  Deal **7 cards** to each player.
3.  Place the remaining cards face down as the draw pile.
4.  Reveal one card as the **cutter card**. Its suit identifies the 7
    that can be used to declare a cut.
5.  Keep the cutter card visible throughout the game. It is an
    indicator, not the first card of the discard pile. It may be placed
    beneath the draw pile.

### 2.2 How to play

1.  Players take turns in the agreed direction of play.
2.  On a turn, play one card that matches the **suit or rank** of the
    top card on the discard pile.
3.  An Ace may be played on any card except a 2. When playing an Ace,
    the player names the suit that the next player must follow.
4.  If a player cannot play a card, they must draw one card from the
    draw pile.
5.  If the drawn card can legally be played, the player may play it
    immediately. Otherwise, the turn passes to the next player.
6.  Continue until a player empties their hand or a player declares a
    valid cut.

### 2.3 Special cards

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Card                                Effect
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  **2**                               The next player must draw 2 cards.
                                      If that player has a 2, they may
                                      play it to pass the penalty to the
                                      following player. The draw penalty
                                      accumulates by 2 for each 2 played.
                                      Only the Ace of Spades may cancel
                                      a 2 penalty.

  **8**                               In a two-player game, the player
                                      who plays an 8 gets another play.
                                      If unable to make that play, they
                                      draw according to the normal draw
                                      rule. In a game with more than two
                                      players, the next player is skipped
                                      and play continues in the same
                                      direction.

  **Jack**                            In a two-player game, the player
                                      who plays a Jack gets another play.
                                      If unable to make that play, they
                                      draw according to the normal draw
                                      rule. In a game with more than two
                                      players, the direction of play is
                                      reversed.

  **Ace**                             May be played on any card except a
                                      2. The player asks for the suit

                                      and the next player may play any
                                      card.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2.4 Ending the game by cutting

A player may declare **"cut"** when they play the **7 whose suit matches
the cutter card**.

When a cut is declared:

1.  The game ends immediately.
2.  Every player totals the points of the cards remaining in their hand.
3.  The player with the **lowest hand-point total** wins.

#### Classic card-point values

  Card                         Points
  ---------------------- ------------
  2                                20
  Ace                              15
  Jack                             11
  Queen                            12
  King                             13
  Number cards (3--10)     Face value

If a player empties their hand before anyone declares a cut, that player
wins.

## 3. PicknPlay Super

### 3.1 Equipment and setup

1.  Use a 54-card deck: the standard 52 cards plus **two Jokers**.
2.  Deal **7 cards** to each player.
3.  Place the remaining cards face down as the draw pile.
4.  Reveal one card as the **cutter card**. Its suit identifies the 7
    that can be used to declare a cut.
5.  Keep the cutter card visible throughout the game. It is an
    indicator, not the first card of the discard pile. It may be placed
    beneath the draw pile.

### 3.2 How to play

Use the same turn sequence and matching rules as PicknPlay Classic:

1.  Play a card matching the suit or rank of the top discard, or play an
    Ace according to its special rule.
2.  If unable to play, draw one card.
3.  If the drawn card is playable, it may be played immediately.
4.  Apply the special-card effects below whenever the relevant card is
    played.

### 3.3 Special cards

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Card                                Effect
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  **2**                               The next player must draw 2 cards.
                                      A player holding another 2 may play
                                      it to pass the penalty onward. The
                                      penalty accumulates by 2 for each 2
                                      played. **Only the Ace of
                                      Spades** may cancel a 2 penalty.

  **3**                               The next player must draw 3 cards.
                                      A player holding another 3 may play
                                      it to pass the penalty onward. The
                                      penalty accumulates by 3 for each 3
                                      played. **Only the Black Spade
                                      Ace** cancels a 3 penalty.

  **Joker**                           The next player must draw 5 cards.
                                      A player holding another Joker may
                                      play it to pass the penalty onward.
                                      The penalty accumulates by 5 for
                                      each Joker played. **Only the Black
                                      Spade Ace** cancels a Joker
                                      penalty.

  **8**                               In a two-player game, the player
                                      who plays an 8 gets another play.
                                      If unable to make that play, they
                                      draw according to the normal draw
                                      rule. In a game with more than two
                                      players, the next player is skipped
                                      and play continues in the same
                                      direction.

  **Jack**                            In a two-player game, the player
                                      who plays a Jack gets another play.
                                      If unable to make that play, they
                                      draw according to the normal draw
                                      rule. In a game with more than two
                                      players, the direction of play is
                                      reversed.

  **Ace**                             May be played on any card except a
                                      2. The player asks for the suit
                                      for the next turn. Only the Ace of
                                      Spades cancels a penalty.

  **Black Spade Ace**                 Has a special value of 60 points
                                      when scoring a cut. It is the only
                                      card that cancels a penalty caused
                                      by a 2, 3, or Joker. A cancelling
                                      Black Spade Ace asks for no suit:
                                      the next player may play any card.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Penalty stacking:** A matching penalty card passes the accumulated
draw penalty to the next player. For example, two 3s create a 6-card
penalty, and two Jokers create a 10-card penalty. The Black Spade Ace
cancels the whole accumulated penalty, so the player does not draw those
cards. It does not ask for a suit, so the next player may play any card.

### 3.4 Ending the game by cutting

A player may declare **"cut"** when they play the **7 whose suit matches
the cutter card**.

When a cut is declared:

1.  The game ends immediately.
2.  Every player totals the points of the cards remaining in their hand.
3.  The player with the **lowest hand-point total** wins.

For PicknPlay Super, the supplied rules specify that a cut is based on a
hand value **below 60**. The Black Spade Ace is worth 60 points.

#### Super card-point values

  Card                         Points
  ---------------------- ------------
  2                                20
  Black Spade Ace                  60
  Other Aces                       15
  Jack                             11
  Queen                            12
  King                             13
  Number cards (3--10)     Face value

If a player empties their hand before anyone declares a cut, that player
wins.

## 5. Important rule notes

-   **The cutter card is separate from the discard pile.** Keep it
    visible so all players can identify the suit required for a cut.
-   **A cut is different from going out.** Going out means a player has
    no cards left. Cutting ends the game so that players compare the
    points left in their hands.
-   **Lowest hand value wins a cut.** Count the points of every card
    remaining in each player's hand.
-   **Only the Black Spade Ace cools off a penalty.** In both variants
    it is the only card that cancels a 2, 3 or Joker penalty. Other Aces
    cannot answer a penalty. After the cool-off no suit is asked for, so
    the next player may play any card.

### Items to agree before play

The supplied rules do not fully define the following details. Players
should agree on them before starting:

1.  **Cut threshold in PicknPlay Super:** The source states that cutting is
    "below 60 card value", but does not clearly state whether this means
    the player declaring the cut must have fewer than 60 points in hand,
    or whether it is another eligibility condition. Confirm the intended
    interpretation.
2.  **Penalty-card response:** The source explains passing and
    accumulating penalties, but does not specify whether a player who
    cannot add a matching penalty card must draw the full accumulated
    amount immediately or whether any other response is allowed.
3.  **Empty draw pile:** The source does not specify how to continue
    when the draw pile runs out. Agree whether to reshuffle the discard
    pile, leaving its top card in place, or use another house rule.
4.  **Starting discard:** The source identifies the cutter card
    separately but does not specify which card starts the discard pile.
    Agree on the opening-discard procedure before play.
5.  **Multiple players tied on the lowest cut score:** The source does
    not specify a tie-break procedure.

## 6. Winning

There are two ways to win:

-   **Go out:** Be the first player to play every card in your hand.
-   **Cut:** Play the 7 matching the cutter card's suit, declare "cut",
    and win by having the lowest remaining hand-point total, subject to
    the agreed PicknPlay Super cut threshold.
